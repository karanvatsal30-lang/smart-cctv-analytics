Core Principles Enforced:

  • Camera-Wide Independence: People counting operates across the entire camera view and is never restricted by regions.
  • Shelf Obstruction Separation: A person in front of a shelf is strictly treated as an obstruction; shelf comparisons are paused until the person leaves.
  • Honest Uncertainty: Under heavy occlusion or blur, the system outputs ranges (e.g. 4–6) or says "Unable to determine reliably" rather than guessing.
  • Zero Speculative Features: No heat maps, no face recognition, no employee/customer role guessing, no theft claims.
